Questions

How does the free trial work?

Choose a plan and start your trial. For 2 days you can control one airport of your choice, free. Cancel from your account before the trial ends and you pay nothing. Otherwise your plan starts, the rest of its airports unlock, and it renews monthly until you cancel. The trial is available once per player.

How do I choose my airports?

After you subscribe, open your account and tick the airports your plan includes. You can fill empty places at any time. Swapping an airport out is free during the trial and then once every 30 days. Unlimited includes every airport, including new ones as they open.

What happens when a new airport opens?

On a plan with a set number of airports, you choose. Swap the new airport in for one of yours from your account (one swap a month). Or keep everything and move up a plan with the new airport’s code: 50% off your new plan for the first 3 months, shown on your account when it opens. Echo already includes every new airport.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. Open your account and choose Manage billing to move up or down a plan, update your card, download invoices or cancel. A cancelled plan stays active until the end of the month you have paid for.

How does commissioning an airport work?

Sign in, send us the airport from the Request page and save a card with our payment provider, Stripe. Nothing is taken then. We check it can be built from published charts; if it cannot, we tell you and nothing is ever charged. When it is built we release it to you and take a one-off £25 from the saved card, with no subscription needed. It is then yours to control for good, whatever plan you are on, and only you can control it for its first month before it opens to subscribers. You can withdraw any time before release.

What is free?

The website, every airport briefing, the Academy reading, the airport map and your career logbook. Opening a position at an airport needs a trial or a plan.

Is this real air traffic control training?

No. Clearway is a simulation game built from published charts. It is not approved for real-world training or navigation and is not affiliated with any air navigation service provider, airport or airline.

These terms cover your use of Clearway (the website at and its simulators). By creating an account or subscribing you agree to them.

The service

Clearway is an air traffic control simulation game. It is built from published aeronautical information and open map data, simplified for play. It must not be used for real-world navigation, flight planning or controller training, and it is not affiliated with any air navigation service provider, airport or airline.

Accounts

You sign in with your email address. Keep access to that email secure: anyone who can read it can sign in as you. One account is for one person.

Subscriptions and the free trial

Plans are billed monthly in advance through our payment provider, Stripe, and renew automatically until you cancel. New players can try one airport of their choice free for 2 days, once, on the plan they choose; if you do not cancel before the trial ends, the first monthly payment is taken then and the rest of the plan’s airports unlock. You can change plan, update your card or cancel at any time from your account. Cancelling stops the next renewal and your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. Prices include any VAT that applies and may change with at least 30 days’ notice by email.

Your right to cancel

If you are a consumer in the UK or EU you normally have 14 days to cancel a new subscription. Because Clearway gives you access to digital content straight away, you agree when subscribing that access starts immediately and that you lose that right once it has started, except where the law says otherwise. The free trial gives you time to decide before paying. If something has gone wrong, contact us and we will look at a refund.

Airports and early access

Your plan lets you open the number of airports it includes. Airports in development, offered through early access, are unfinished and may change or be withdrawn. We may update, improve or retire features; if we withdraw an airport you have chosen you can pick another. When a new airport opens you can swap it in for one of your chosen airports (one swap a month), or move up a plan using that airport’s offer code, which gives the stated discount on your new plan for the stated number of months and then renews at the normal price.

Commissioned airports

You can ask us to build an airport for a one-off price, shown when you ask. When you ask, you save a card with Stripe and authorise us to charge that price to it once, when the airport is built and released to you. Nothing is taken before then, and you can withdraw at any time before release. We may decline a request, for example if there is not enough published information to build it; nothing is charged in that case. If the charge needs your confirmation or fails, the airport waits on your account until you pay. A paid airport is added to your account for as long as Clearway runs it, whatever plan you are on, including none. For its first month only you can control it; after that we may open it to other players. It is built to the same standard as other Clearway airports and may be updated like them. Because it is made to your request and you can play it straight away, the 14-day right to cancel does not apply once you have paid and it is unlocked, except where the law says otherwise.

Privacy

Last updated .

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address, your plan, the airports you have chosen and when your trial and billing period end.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number; Stripe tells us your plan’s status.
  • Feedback and airport requests: what you write, the page you were on, your browser type and, if you give it or are signed in, your email so we can reply. Anonymous senders are recorded only as a one-way hash, to prevent spam.
  • Commissioned airports: the airport you asked for, your notes, its progress and when you paid.
  • Your career logbook and settings stay in your browser and are not sent to us.

Who processes it

Supabase (accounts and database), Stripe (payments), Vercel (our server functions) and GitHub Pages (the website). Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap, and live traffic and weather from public aviation data feeds; those requests include your IP address, as any web request does.

Cookies and analytics

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Signing in stores a session token in your browser’s local storage.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or for your account and data to be deleted, at any time. Cancel any subscription first, then contact us. We keep payment records for as long as tax law requires.
