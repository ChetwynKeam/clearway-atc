Questions

How does the free trial work?

Choose a plan and start your trial. Nothing is charged for 2 days, and you can cancel from your account before then without paying anything. After the trial the plan renews monthly until you cancel. The trial is available once per player.

How do I choose my airports?

After you subscribe, open your account and tick the airports your plan includes. You can fill empty places at any time. Swapping an airport out is free during the trial and then once every 30 days. Unlimited includes every airport, including new ones as they open.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. Open your account and choose Manage billing to move up or down a plan, update your card, download invoices or cancel. A cancelled plan stays active until the end of the month you have paid for.

What is free?

The website, every airport briefing, the Academy reading, the airport map and your career logbook. Opening a position at an airport needs a trial or a plan.

Is this real air traffic control training?

No. Clearway is a simulation game built from published charts. It is not approved for real-world training or navigation and is not affiliated with any air navigation service provider, airport or airline.

It must not be used for real-world navigation, flight planning or controller training, and it is not affiliated with any air navigation service provider, airport or airline.

Accounts

Subscriptions and the free trial

New players can try a plan free for 2 days once; if you do not cancel before the trial ends, the first monthly payment is taken then. You can change plan, update your card or cancel at any time from your account. Cancelling stops the next renewal and your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. Prices include any VAT that applies and may change with at least 30 days’ notice by email.

The free trial gives you time to decide before paying. If something has gone wrong, contact us and we will look at a refund.

Privacy

Last updated .

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address, your plan, the airports you have chosen and when your trial and billing period end.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number; Stripe tells us your plan’s status.
  • Feedback and airport requests: what you write, the page you were on, your browser type and, if you give it or are signed in, your email so we can reply. Anonymous senders are recorded only as a one-way hash, to prevent spam.
  • Your career logbook and settings stay in your browser and are not sent to us.

Who processes it

Supabase (accounts and database), Stripe (payments), Vercel (our server functions) and GitHub Pages (the website). Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap, and live traffic and weather from public aviation data feeds; those requests include your IP address, as any web request does.

Cookies and analytics

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Signing in stores a session token in your browser’s local storage.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or for your account and data to be deleted, at any time. Cancel any subscription first, then contact us. We keep payment records for as long as tax law requires.
